## Dynamic pricing

Every agency has a **target stock** per item — the "ideal" amount. When actual stock deviates from that target, prices move automatically via a power-law formula to push stock back toward target.

- **High stock** (above target) → prices drop (incentivize sales out)
- **Low stock** (below target) → prices climb (incentivize inflow)
- The formula is configurable per item: floor/ceiling bounds, sensitivity (how fast it reacts), decay (how much stock is "forgotten" per cycle)
- Price snapshots are captured hourly for trend sparklines in the Dashboard

Stock is recomputed immediately after every Sell or Buy.

## Sell workflow

1. Player interacts with agency NPC and selects items/quantity
2. System shows the current sell price and expected payout (after commission, before tax)
3. **If staff are on duty** for the agency's job → sale goes to pending queue
4. **If no staff or pending times out** → instant NPC sale (with same price, same commission)
5. Staff review and accept/reject from `/depending` or the Pending NUI
6. On acceptance: payout is withdrawn from agency's job account, commission and tax are calculated

The player is always offered the sell-side of the price spread, not the midpoint.

## Buy workflow

1. Player opens a cart interface and browses available items
2. Adds multiple items with quantities, sees a running total, applies tax estimate
3. **Checkout creates a pending order** — always. No NPC fallback, no timeout.
4. If staff are on-duty, they're notified; otherwise the buyer is told to wait
5. Staff review the order and pick a **purchase type**:
   - **Instansi** (buyer has the job) → price gets a per-agency discount
   - **Gang/non-instansi** (buyer doesn't) → price gets a per-agency markup
6. On acceptance: revenue lands in the agency's job account, optional printable receipt is queued to `dnd_jobmanagement`

Staff see the adjusted price (with discount/markup applied) when reviewing, so they know what the player will actually pay.

## Price spread (instansi vs gang)

When staff accept a Buy order, the purchase-type choice determines the final price:

- **Instansi discount** — buyers with the agency's job see a lower price (e.g., 4% off mid-price). Internal procurement is incentivized.
- **Gang markup** — buyers without the job see a higher price (e.g., 6% markup). Outside sales are more profitable for the agency.

Both are **per-agency and dashboard-editable**, so each agency can tune its own margins.

## Agency budgets

Agencies are no longer infinite wallets. When staff accept a Sell, the payout is **withdrawn from the agency's job account**. The agency must have sufficient balance **and** stay within its spending quota.

- **Balance constraint** — hard limit, can't pay if the account is empty
- **Quota constraint** — soft limit, market closes when agency hits its per-cycle spending cap; reopens at cycle boundary

Per-agency overrides allow fine-tuning enforcement and quota settings from the Dashboard.

See [Agency Budget System](./Guides/budget-system) for details.

## Transaction tax and stash upkeep

- **Transaction tax** — a percentage of every Sell/Buy is collected (separate rates for each). Tax is routed to a government job account (treasury) or destroyed (money sink).
- **Stash upkeep** — hourly charge for holding inventory (flat fee + per-unit fee). Prevents hoarding stock to suppress prices.

Both are **optional** (can be disabled) and both are **reconfigurable** from the Dashboard (for government-access users).

See [Transaction Tax & Upkeep](./Guides/tax-system) for details.

## Dashboard

A game-facing NUI for agency managers and government. Accessible via `/dedashboard`. Features:

### Agency manager view (per-agency)
- **Stock & pricing** — current price, target stock, and live pricing curve for each item
- **Price holds** — temporarily lock a price at a specific level (during events, sales, etc.)
- **Transactions** — recent Sell/Buy history, grouped by order, expandable to line items
- **Discord webhook** — log significant price changes to Discord in real-time
- **Buy price adjustment** — instansi discount and gang markup, per-agency
- **Budget status** — current balance, spending this cycle, quota limit, time to reset

### Government view (cross-agency)
- Bird's-eye view of all agencies
- Budget overview (low-balance alerts, quotas reached)
- Tax collected, upkeep charged
- Treasury account balance

All dashboard edits take effect live — no restart needed.

## Market overview

A read-only NUI (`/demarket`) showing every agency at a glance:

- Current price per item
- Stock level and demand label (high/balanced/low)
- Optional: actual stock numbers (if agency has `publicStock = true`)

Players can use this to comparison-shop between agencies.

## Pending orders

The `/depending` command (or the Pending NUI) shows:

- **Sell requests** — pending Sell orders awaiting staff acceptance, with countdown until NPC fallback
- **Buy orders** — pending Buy orders awaiting staff acceptance, no timeout

Staff can accept/reject individually, with optional price/quantity adjustments before acceptance.

## Optional integrations

### Renewed-Banking

If installed and enabled (`Config.Banking.Enabled = true`):

- Buy revenue deposits directly into the agency's job account (not a personal balance)
- Agency budget enforcement reads/writes the job account balance
- Tax is transferred to the treasury job account
- Stash upkeep is charged from the job account

Without it, Buy is disabled and budgets can't enforce spending.

### dnd_jobmanagement

If installed and enabled (`Config.Printer = 'dnd_jobmanagement'`):

- When staff accept a Buy order, a printable purchase document is auto-queued to the printer system
- Document shows buyer, items, quantities, price-per-unit, total, and any applicable discount/markup

Without it, sales complete normally — the printout just doesn't happen.

### dnd_phone

If installed:

- Bank-paid transactions (when `Config.MoneyAccount = 'bank'`) are logged to the player's mBanking history
- Shows up on the player's in-game phone, not just their balance

Without it, transactions still work — they just don't appear in the phone's history.

## Configuration-driven

Everything is tuned from `config.lua`:

- Item price floors/ceilings, sensitivity, decay
- Agency job, commission, stash location
- Budget limits and quotas
- Tax rates and destination
- Upkeep fees
- Color theming (accent colors, no CSS edits needed)
- Command names (avoid conflicts with other resources)

A few runtime fields (webhook URLs, dashboard edits) are **intentionally excluded** from the config sync so they survive a restart — see [Configuration](./Installation/configuration) for the full list.
