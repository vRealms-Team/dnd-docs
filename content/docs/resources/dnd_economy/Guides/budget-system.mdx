title: Agency Budget System

## Overview

In v2.0.0, agencies can no longer conjure money from nowhere. When an agency accepts a Sell order, the payout is **withdrawn from the agency's job account balance**. This creates real economic constraints and makes government a meaningful role.

## How it works

### Two-constraint model

Budget enforcement is subject to **two independent limits**. Both must pass for a sale to succeed:

1. **Balance constraint** — the agency's job account must hold the payout amount (+ staff commission if `commissionFromAgency = true`).
2. **Quota constraint** — the agency's spending this cycle must stay below its per-cycle quota limit.

If either fails, the sale is rejected and the player is told why.

### The quota cycle

A quota is a **soft spending cap per cycle**. When an agency hits its quota, the market for that agency simply closes until the next cycle rolls.

Example: if quota is $2,000,000/24h and the agency hits $2M spent, all Sell attempts are rejected with "Market closed — reopens at [time]". At the cycle boundary, the clock resets and the agency can sell again.

The cycle length is per-agency and dashboard-editable. Default is 24 hours from the first transaction.

### Graceful degradation

If budget enforcement **cannot run** (e.g., `Renewed-Banking` is down or the banking bridge is misconfigured), enforcement is skipped **rather than blocking every sale**. The system logs a warning once per agency and reverts to the old "free money" behavior until the bridge is back.

This is deliberate: the resource staying online with a leaky economy is better than the whole system being dead because one resource went down.

## Configuration

```lua
Config.Budget = {
    Enabled = true,

    enforcedByDefault = true,     -- new agencies start enforced
    quotaLimit        = 2000000,  -- $ per cycle
    quotaCycleHours   = 24,

    commissionFromAgency = true,  -- staff commission also deducted from agency
    lowWarnPct = 0.2,             -- warn dashboard at 20% of quota remaining
}
```

### Per-agency overrides

After first start, each agency can override these defaults from the Dashboard (government-access only):

- `budget_enforced` — toggle enforcement on/off for this agency
- `quota_limit` — this agency's custom quota
- `quota_cycle_hours` — this agency's custom cycle length

These survive restart and take precedence over `Config`.

## Dashboard workflow

A government-access player can see each agency's budget status in the Dashboard:

- **Current balance** — job account balance right now
- **Spent this cycle** — total payout already charged
- **Quota limit** — this cycle's spending cap
- **Time to reset** — when the quota resets (if at limit)
- **Low-balance alert** — red warning when below `lowWarnPct` of quota

If an agency's balance is critically low, government can inject funds directly into the job account through the Dashboard's Budget tab (if they have `Renewed-Banking`'s account management access).

## Staff perspective

From staff's perspective, the change is transparent. They still accept/reject Sell orders. What's new:

- If the agency can't afford a payout, the sale is rejected when they accept it (not before).
- If the agency has hit its quota, the sale is rejected (again, at acceptance time).
- The pending Sell UI shows these rejections clearly: "Agency balance insufficient" or "Quota limit reached this cycle".

## Interaction with Tax and Commission

A Sell transaction involves three money movements:

1. **Seller payout** — withdrawn from agency account
2. **Staff commission** — if `commissionFromAgency = true`, also withdrawn from agency; otherwise, it's a bonus that doesn't cost the agency
3. **Tax** — collected AFTER the transaction completes (if Tax is enabled), routed to its destination

The budget constraint covers items 1 and 2. Item 3 happens after, so tax doesn't "count against" the budget check itself — but it does further drain the agency's balance.

## Money flow diagram

```
Player Sells Item
  ↓
Staff tries to Accept
  ↓
Budget check:
  - Does agency have $X (payout + commission)?
  - Is spending < quota this cycle?
  ↓ [both yes]
Accept succeeds:
  - Payout withdrawn from agency → player
  - Commission withdrawn from agency → staff
  - Price recomputed
  - Tax collected (if enabled) → treasury/burn
  ↓ [either no]
Reject:
  - Nothing moves
  - Player is told why
```
